Corpus Platform Review

Test two corpus platforms. Compare their tools and how well they support research.

Required reading

Akishev, T. (2026). Evaluating corpus interface usability for linguistic research: The case of the Kazakh National Corpus. SKASE Journal of Theoretical Linguistics, 23(1), 209–228.

Read the article (PDF)

Task 1 of 8

Evaluation criteria (Table 1)

From Table 1 in Akishev (2026, p. 214):

  1. Presence or absence. Does the platform have the feature?
  2. Accessibility. Is it easy to find and use?
  3. Functionality. Does it work as expected?
  4. Overall usability for research. Can researchers use it for their work?

In this lab, Task 6 covers features, documentation and research support. In Task 7, you will use the four criteria provided above to evaluate both platforms.
